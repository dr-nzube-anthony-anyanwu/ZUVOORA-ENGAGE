# Commercial Strategy & Go-to-Market

Start in Abuja with eye-care businesses that already have a website and meaningful digital enquiries. Validate demand clinic by clinic.

## Value proposition
Make a clinic website responsive around the clock, help visitors find trusted information and make booking easier.

## Packages
Essentials, Advanced and Full ZUVOORA. Use feature entitlements; avoid separate codebases.

## Illustrative pricing hypotheses — validate before launch
- Essentials setup ₦150k–₦200k; monthly ₦15k–₦25k.
- Professional setup ₦250k–₦350k; monthly ₦35k–₦50k.
- Premium setup ₦450k–₦750k+; monthly ₦65k–₦100k+.

These are not validated market prices. Measure AI, hosting, support, email and optional channel costs. State usage limits and third-party fees clearly.

## Pilot
Up to three clinics: independent optometry practice, medium clinic with scheduling, larger/multi-service clinic. Pilot 6–8 weeks after acceptance. Measure adoption, answer accuracy, handover, confirmed bookings, email reliability, cost and willingness to renew.

## Principles
Sell operational outcomes, not AI novelty. Qualify low-traffic clinics. Setup fee covers onboarding; monthly covers platform and defined support. Reusable modules first; bespoke enterprise work separately priced. Do not guarantee revenue or clinical outcomes.
