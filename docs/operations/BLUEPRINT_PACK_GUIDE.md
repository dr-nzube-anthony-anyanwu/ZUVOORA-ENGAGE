# Important use notes

- Treat these as version 1.0 baselines, not immutable specifications.

- Validate all provider APIs, account-plan restrictions, prices, legal obligations and vendor data-processing terms before implementation or customer commitments.

- Pricing ranges and market assumptions are hypotheses to validate through clinic discovery and paid pilots.

- Do not place real patient data in demos or development environments without an approved lawful basis and safeguards.

- Update the documents when decisions change; maintain a decision log and version history.
